About Pastor Bob

Pastor Bob Kopeny

Senior Pastor
Calvary Chapel East Anaheim

Pastor Bob Kopeny planted Calvary Chapel East Anaheim in 1985 and has served as its senior pastor for over 40 years. He leads a congregation of over 2,500 and is known for his expository, verse-by-verse teaching through the entire Bible.

He attended Biola College and earned his Master of Divinity (M.Div.) from Talbot Theological Seminary.

Calvary Chapel East Anaheim is located at 5605 East La Palma Avenue in Anaheim, California. Services are held Sundays at 9am and 11am, and Wednesdays at 7pm.
